02 / NUTRIENT INTELLIGENCE

Fertilizer recommendations that make sense.

Agro Z prioritizes nutrient need first, then shows suitable fertilizer names for the selected crop. Final doses must follow soil-test and local crop recommendations.

How the recommendation works
1Identify the recommended crop
2Compare soil N-P-K with the crop profile
3Detect Low / Adequate / High nutrients
4Show fertilizer types that address the deficiency
5Cross-check with soil test, crop stage and local RDF before application
Run soil analysis
Safety by design

The app avoids pretending that one brand or one dose is universally “best”. It connects nutrient deficiency to fertilizer type and keeps final dosing site-specific.
